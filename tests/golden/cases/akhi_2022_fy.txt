Independent Auditor's Report

To the Shareholders of Al Khaleej Takaful Islamic Insurance Company Q.P.S.C.

In our opinion, the accompanying financial statements present fairly, in all
material respects, the financial position of the Company as at 31 December 2022,
and its financial performance, in accordance with IFRS as adopted by QCB
(Islamic).

Note: These financial statements comprise two separate income statements — the
Policyholders' Income Statement and the Shareholders' Income Statement — in
accordance with Takaful industry practice. Wakala and Tabarru' transactions are
recorded through the Policyholders' Fund with Participants' surplus distributions
to the Shareholders' Fund recognised as Wakala fees under Qard Hassan framework.
